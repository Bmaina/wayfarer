# Vercel X application: draft answers (edit before submitting; close 29 Nov 2026, 11:59pm PT)

Stage to select: **Prototype** (do not select Launched or Generating revenue).

**What are you building?**
WAYFARER is a team of AI agents that plans, prices, verifies and requests trips for international travellers visiting East Africa. A traveller describes the trip and budget; agents compare an assembled itinerary against supplier packages, check budget and logistics, and prepare supplier requests. A deterministic engine does all arithmetic, and a person approves every payment.

**What problem are you aiming to solve, and who is it for?**
Planning a multi-lodge safari means dealing with several suppliers, park fees and internal flights, with no easy way to tell whether a package is good value. WAYFARER is for US and European leisure travellers spending roughly $5,000 to $15,000 per party, and for the East African operators who currently spend heavily to win each booking.

**Why are you uniquely positioned?**
[Edit] 15+ years in geospatial and satellite data, based in Nairobi with field experience across East Africa, an MBA (Gies) and an MS in Geoinformation and Earth Observation (Twente), and PMP certified. I can build and I am on the ground where suppliers are.

**What do you plan to validate by Demo Day?** (commit only to what you will do)
At least 25 qualified trip briefs, 5 suppliers willing to quote through WAYFARER, 3 verified proposals sent to real travellers, and one paid deposit or booking-ready customer. Decision rule: if realised margin is under 9% or acquisition cost over 8% of booked value, pivot to an operator-facing front desk.
